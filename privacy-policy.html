Privacy Policy & Data Governance

Last updated: October 2, 2026

At SearchForge SEO, we adhere to strict data privacy principles in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

1. Privacy-Conscious Analytics (F20 Compliance)

We employ privacy-preserving telemetry to measure Core Web Vitals and site usability. We do not store personal identifiable information (PII) without explicit user consent, nor do we sell diagnostic crawl logs to third-party ad networks.

2. Diagnostic Audit Data

When submitting URLs through our contact forms or diagnostic tools, technical data collected (server response headers, public robots.txt files, and rendered DOM structures) is used solely to generate your technical audit report.
